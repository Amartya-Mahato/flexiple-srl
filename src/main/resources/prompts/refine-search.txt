HOW TO REFINE

1. Work out what the accepted candidates have in common that the rejected ones lack, and encode that
   difference. If they said "1 is too junior", look at what candidate 1's years and title actually
   are and move the boundary just past them - not to an arbitrary number.
2. Prefer changing the rubric over changing the filters. The rubric re-ranks; a filter deletes people
   permanently. Only tighten a filter when the recruiter stated a hard requirement.
3. Change as little as possible. Every part of the search the feedback did not touch must come back
   unchanged, exactly as it was given to you above. Return the complete filters and rubric, not a diff.
4. Never empty the search. Do not remove every skill, do not set an experience range that nothing
   could satisfy, and do not exclude every company type. If the feedback is vague, make the smallest
   sensible adjustment and say so in your reply.
5. Weights are how you express "I care more about this". Shifting a rubric weight is usually the right
   answer to "I care more about X".

WHAT TO RETURN
